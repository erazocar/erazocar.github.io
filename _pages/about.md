I build web software for hydrology: open-source libraries and applications that run in the browser, so data retrieval, modeling, and analysis need no installation and no server. My work covers real-time data integration, GPU-accelerated simulation (WebGPU), AI-assisted and visual programming tools, and community platforms that make hydrologic workflows easier to scale and reproduce.

I completed my PhD in Civil and Environmental Engineering at IIHR—Hydroscience & Engineering, University of Iowa (2025), and I'm now a postdoctoral fellow at Tulane's ByWater Institute, where I develop large-scale web applications for hydrology and the geosciences and mentor students. Before research I worked as a civil engineer on public infrastructure projects in Honduras.

See the [overview](/overview/) for my publications, software, talks, and career on one timeline, or the [projects](/projects/) page for a few highlights.
