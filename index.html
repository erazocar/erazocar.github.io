Carlos Erazo Ramirez

Postdoctoral Fellow, ByWater Institute | Hydroinformatics Lab, Tulane University

I’m a postdoctoral fellow at Tulane’s ByWater Institute. My work advances scientific computing through open-source software for hydrology and environmental modeling: web-native environments that bring numerical simulation, high-performance and GPU computing, data interoperability, rigorous model evaluation and AI-assisted workflows to the browser. This spans computational models, evaluation frameworks that examine predictive accuracy, uncertainty and physical realism across spatial scales, visual programming systems for reproducible analyses and, more recently, agentic architectures that connect methods, data and tools through structured workflows with explicit validation and provenance. I believe scientific software should be accessible, extensible, transparent and reusable, so researchers and engineers can build on existing capabilities instead of recreating the same infrastructure. I hold a PhD from IIHR—Hydroscience & Engineering, University of Iowa, and an Erasmus Mundus MSc in hydroinformatics, and I worked as a civil engineer in Honduras before research.
