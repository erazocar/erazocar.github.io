HydroClaw is a study workspace that sits between language-model agents and hydrological compute tools. The agent scopes the question, chooses among planned options and explains results. HydroClaw fetches the data, runs established and validated methods in private environments, records the provenance of every number, and assembles the manuscript's computational sections from the run records.
